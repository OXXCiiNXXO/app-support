适用范围

本政策适用于周培臣 / CiiN 开发的《狗赶羊》（Sheep Dog），说明游戏如何处理存档、所选照片、购买状态以及您主动发送的内容。游戏无需注册开发者账号，没有开发者运营的游戏服务器、广告、行为分析或第三方崩溃上报 SDK。

日常游玩和本地保存可离线进行。iCloud 同步、App Store 价格查询、购买与恢复购买需要连接 Apple 服务；您选中的照片如果仅在 iCloud 照片中，系统也可能需要联网下载。天气在游戏内生成，不读取您的现实位置。

本机保存的内容

游戏在本机保存羊群与羊舍状态、牧场布置、植物与天气进度、花环与收藏、旅行纪念、委托进度、绒毛、制作与技能学习记录、试用次数、克隆羊品种与名字，以及语言、音量和操作偏好。这些内容用于继续游戏和展示您的牧场。

克隆羊成品图片保存在应用自己的文件目录。明信片预览和分享文件会临时保存在本机；应用会清理自己的旧临时导出文件，不会因此删除您已存入系统照片图库的图片。

选照片制作克隆羊

只有您打开系统照片选择器并选中图片后，游戏才会读取那张图片来制作自定义羊种。游戏不要求整个照片图库的读取权限，不扫描未选中的图片，也不使用相机或麦克风。

缩放、涂抹保留或擦除背景、裁切和透明 PNG 生成均在设备上完成。确认制作成功后，游戏保存成品 PNG 和您输入的品种名字，不将原始照片或整个图库作为牧场存档上传。成品 PNG 从处理后的像素重新生成，不复制原始照片的拍摄位置等 EXIF 信息。

启用本应用的系统 iCloud 功能后，成品 PNG、名字和品种进度会随牧场存档同步到您自己的 iCloud 私有空间。照片可能包含个人信息，请选择您愿意用于牧场的图片。

可选的 iCloud 牧场存档

在系统中登录 iCloud 并允许本应用使用 iCloud 后，游戏会自动尝试同步牧场进度。无需额外游戏登录，也不收取存档费用。未登录、关闭本应用的 iCloud 功能或暂时离线时，仍可在本机继续游玩。

同步内容包括各模式存档、收藏与布置、绒毛及制作记录、永久学习与试用进度、克隆羊图片和名字、部分操作偏好，以及用于辨认存档的保存时间与设备类别（如 iPhone 或 iPad）。语言和背景音乐开关保留为设备偏好。Apple 提供的应用范围账户标识用于区分同步账户，不是您的 Apple 账户密码。

这些内容保存在 Apple CloudKit 的用户私有数据库，供同一 iCloud 账户恢复牧场。开发者没有读取其他玩家私有存档或克隆照片的管理访问权限，游戏也没有公开牧场数据库。Apple 的处理方式见 iCloud 与隐私，数据库访问方式见 Apple CloudKit 说明。

两台设备各自出现新进度时，游戏会让您回到首页后选择继续本机或云端的一整份存档，不自动拼接资源。替换前会在当前设备保留最近一次被替换存档的副本，可通过 iCloud 存档页找回。正在游玩的牧场不会被云端直接替换。

购买、分享与联系

三个永久内容包由 Apple App Store 处理购买。游戏通过 StoreKit 读取并验证购买、恢复及撤销状态，以决定内容访问权限。开发者不接收您的支付卡信息或 Apple 账户密码，不设自营交易服务器。购买记录与 iCloud 牧场进度分别恢复。有关 Apple 的处理方式，见 App Store 与隐私。

只有您主动选择分享、保存明信片或联系开发者时，游戏才会打开系统分享界面、请求添加照片权限或打开对应应用/网站。分享图片可能包含您制作的克隆羊或输入的名字，请在发送前确认。接收方和相应服务按各自的隐私政策处理您发送的内容。

如果您发送支持邮件，我们会收到您的邮件地址、邮件内容及您主动附上的截图或诊断说明，用于回应问题。请不要发送支付卡、账号密码或不必要的私人照片。可通过 oxxciinxxo@gmail.com 请求处理您发送给我们的支持资料。

管理、保留与删除

重新开始一局会保留长期收藏、已学习技能和克隆品种，并不等于清除全部资料。要删除本机应用资料，请使用系统“删除 App”，而非仅移除主屏幕图标或“卸载 App”。这不会自动删除 iCloud 副本、Apple 购买记录，或您已保存/分享到其他应用的图片。

可在系统设置 → 您的名字 → iCloud 中管理本应用的同步；关闭同步不会主动清除已经存在的云端副本。要删除云端数据，请在 iCloud 的存储管理中选择本应用并使用系统提供的删除选项。各系统版本的名称可能不同，见 Apple 的 iCloud 设置说明。

如要移除所有设备与云端的牧场副本，请先停止各设备上的本应用同步，再删除各设备的本机资料与 iCloud 中的本应用数据。保有旧档的设备重新开启同步后，可能再次上传那份旧档。系统设备备份和您导出的图片需在相应系统或应用中另行管理。

没有独立的游戏账号可供删除。开发者无法替您读取或直接操作其他玩家的私有 iCloud 存档；如不确定系统中的删除入口，欢迎联系支持。

Scope

This policy covers Sheep Dog (狗赶羊), developed by 周培臣 / CiiN, and explains its saves, selected photos, purchase status, and content you choose to send. The game requires no developer account and has no developer-operated game server, advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Ordinary play and local saving work offline. iCloud sync, App Store prices, purchases, and purchase restoration use Apple services. A photo you select may also need to be downloaded by the system from iCloud Photos. Weather is generated within the game and does not use your real location.

Data on your device

The game saves flock and sheepfold state, meadow layouts, plant and weather progress, wreaths and collections, travel memories, jobs, wool, crafting and helper-learning records, trial uses, custom breeds and names, and language, audio, and control preferences. These support continuing and displaying your meadow.

Finished custom-breed images are stored in the app’s own directory. Postcard previews and share files are temporarily stored locally. The app cleans up its own old exports; this does not delete pictures you have already saved to the system Photos library.

Photos for custom breeds

The game reads an image for a custom breed only after you open the system Photos picker and select it. It does not request read access to your entire library, scan unselected pictures, or use the camera or microphone.

Resizing, brushing areas to keep or erase, cropping, and transparent PNG creation happen on your device. After a successful craft, the game stores the finished PNG and your breed name. It does not upload your original photo or whole library as a meadow save. The finished PNG is generated from the edited pixels without copying source EXIF metadata such as capture location.

When the app’s iCloud feature is enabled in system settings, the finished PNG, name, and breed progress sync with your meadow to your private iCloud space. Images can contain personal information, so choose pictures you are comfortable using in your meadow.

Optional iCloud saves

When you are signed in to iCloud and allow the app to use it in system settings, the game automatically attempts to sync progress. No separate game login or save subscription is needed. You can keep playing locally if signed out, if the app’s iCloud access is off, or while offline.

Sync includes mode saves, collections and layouts, wool and crafting records, permanent learning and trial progress, custom-breed images and names, some controls, and a save time and device category such as iPhone or iPad. Language and background-music settings stay on each device. An app-scoped account identifier from Apple distinguishes sync accounts; it is not your Apple Account password.

The data is stored in your private Apple CloudKit database to restore the meadow using the same iCloud account. The developer has no administrative access to read other players’ private saves or custom photos, and the game has no public meadow database. See iCloud & Privacy and Apple’s CloudKit explanation.

If both devices have new progress, return to the title screen and choose one complete local or cloud save. Resources are not automatically merged. The most recently displaced save is kept as a backup on the current device and can be recovered from the iCloud save page. A cloud save never directly replaces a meadow during active play.

Purchases, sharing, and support

Apple’s App Store handles the three permanent content packs. The app uses StoreKit to read and verify purchases, restoration, and revocations to determine access. The developer receives neither your payment-card details nor Apple Account password and runs no transaction server. Purchase restoration is separate from iCloud progress restoration. See App Store & Privacy.

The game opens the system share sheet, requests permission to add a postcard to Photos, or opens a contact app or website only when you choose that action. Shared pictures may include your custom breeds or names; review them before sending. The recipient and selected service handle the content under their own privacy policies.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to attach so we can respond. Do not send payment cards, passwords, or unnecessary private pictures. Contact oxxciinxxo@gmail.com about support information you have sent us.

Control, retention, and deletion

Starting another round keeps long-term collections, learned helpers, and custom breeds; it is not a full data reset. To remove local app data, use the system’s Delete App action, rather than removing its Home Screen icon or offloading it. This does not automatically delete the iCloud copy, Apple purchase records, or images already saved or shared elsewhere.

Manage the app’s sync in Settings → your name → iCloud. Turning sync off does not actively erase an existing cloud copy. To delete cloud data, select this app in iCloud storage management and use the system’s deletion option. Labels vary by system version; see Apple’s iCloud settings guide.

To remove meadow copies from all devices and iCloud, first stop this app’s sync on those devices, then delete the local app data and the app’s iCloud data. A device retaining an old save may upload it again if sync is re-enabled. System device backups and exported pictures must be managed separately in the relevant system or app.

There is no separate game account to delete. The developer cannot read or directly manage other players’ private iCloud saves. Contact support if you need help finding the system controls.
