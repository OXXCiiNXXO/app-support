Summary

SheepDogGame (the "App") is a fully offline iOS casual game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data whatsoever. All game progress is saved locally on your device only and never uploaded to any server.

Data Collected

None. Specifically:

  • ❌ No device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera permissions
  • ❌ No third-party analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Adjust, etc.)
  • ❌ No ad SDKs (AdMob, Unity Ads, IronSource, etc.)
  • ❌ No crash reporting SDKs (Sentry, Crashlytics, etc.)
  • ❌ No network requests of any kind (no server, no API, no cloud sync)

Local Storage (UserDefaults only)

The App stores the following non-personal game-progress data exclusively on your device via UserDefaults:

Data ItemPurposeHow to Clear
Sheep positions / velocities / breeds / agesRestore flock state on continueDelete app
Dog position / velocityRestore dog state on continueDelete app
Rock positions / sizesRestore map decorationsDelete app
Tree positions / growth stage / timersRestore tree lifecycleDelete app
Statistic counters (breeding / collision counts)Show cumulative stats on title screenDelete app
Canvas size cacheAdapt to different screen sizesDelete app

These data are non-personal, never leave your device, never shared with any third party, and are permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

None. Zero external dependencies. Built entirely with Apple-native frameworks (SwiftUI, Foundation, UIKit).

Children's Privacy

This App is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, it automatically complies with COPPA and similar regulations. No parental consent is required.

Data Security

  • No network transmission = no man-in-the-middle risk
  • No server storage = no data-breach risk
  • Local data is isolated by the iOS sandbox; other apps cannot access it

Your Rights

Because we collect no data, these rights are automatically satisfied:

  • Right to know → this policy is the complete notice
  • Right to access → nothing to access
  • Right to rectification → nothing to correct
  • Right to deletion → uninstalling the app deletes all local data
  • Right to portability → nothing to portable
  • Right to withdraw consent → no consent was ever requested

Changes to This Policy

If a future version ever begins collecting data (which is highly unlikely given our purely offline design), we will:

  1. Mark it prominently in the app update notes
  2. Update this privacy policy and notify users within the app
  3. Obtain your explicit consent before any collection begins

概述

SheepDogGame（以下简称"本应用"或"我们"）是一款完全离线运行的 iOS 休闲游戏。我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人身份信息或使用数据。所有游戏数据仅保存在您的设备本地，绝不上传至任何服务器。

我们收集的数据

无。 本应用不收集任何数据。具体而言：

  • ❌ 不收集设备标识符（IDFA/IDFV）
  • ❌ 不收集位置信息
  • ❌ 不收集联系人、照片、麦克风、相机等权限数据
  • ❌ 不使用任何第三方分析 SDK（如 Firebase Analytics、AppsFlyer、Adjust 等）
  • ❌ 不使用任何广告 SDK（如 AdMob、Unity Ads、IronSource 等）
  • ❌ 不使用任何崩溃上报 SDK（如 Sentry、Crashlytics 等）
  • ❌ 不进行任何网络请求（无服务器、无 API、无云同步）

本地存储的数据

本应用仅在您的设备本地（通过 UserDefaults）保存以下非个人游戏进度数据：

数据项用途是否可清除

第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方服务、SDK、框架或 API。 完全基于 Apple 原生框架（SwiftUI、Foundation、UIKit）开发，零外部依赖。

儿童隐私

本应用面向全年龄段用户。由于我们完全不收集任何数据，自动符合《儿童在线隐私保护法案》（COPPA）及同类法规要求。无需家长同意即可安全使用。
