QuackFeed Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

QuackFeed respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles data.

1. Data collection

The developer does not collect, store, sell, or track personal data. Gameplay, scores, and preferences are processed on your device.

2. On-device storage

QuackFeed stores game records, story progress, achievements, language, and accessibility preferences locally. You can remove this local data by deleting the app. Tilt controls process device motion locally and do not upload it. This information does not identify you and is not sent to the developer.

3. Apple services and external links

QuackFeed does not use analytics tools, third-party advertising SDKs, or tracking services. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store and are subject to Apple's privacy policy. If you choose an external developer or contact link, the corresponding service opens outside QuackFeed.

4. Children

QuackFeed does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

5. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions may be sent to oxxciinxxo@gmail.com.

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 8 日

QuackFeed 尊重并保护您的隐私。本政策说明 App 的数据处理方式。

1. 数据收集

开发者不会收集、存储、出售或追踪任何个人数据。游戏、成绩和偏好均在您的设备上处理。

2. 设备本地存储

App 仅在设备上保存游戏成绩、剧情进度、成就、语言和辅助功能偏好。删除 App 可以移除这些本地数据。倾斜操作使用的设备运动数据仅在本机处理，不会上传。这些信息不用于识别您，也不会发送给开发者。

3. Apple 服务与外部链接

App 不使用分析工具、第三方广告 SDK 或跟踪服务。应用内购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 和 App Store 处理，并受 Apple 隐私政策约束。只有当您主动点击开发者或联系方式时，才会离开 QuackFeed 并打开相应服务。

4. 儿童隐私

QuackFeed 不会有意收集儿童或其他用户的个人数据。

5. 变更与联系

本政策如有更新，将发布在本页面。如有疑问，请联系 oxxciinxxo@gmail.com。
