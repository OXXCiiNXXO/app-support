FAQ

Are these ads real?

No! All ad content is fictional satire meant for entertainment. If you really want those flying slippers, physics will need to make some breakthroughs first.

Why do some ads look so realistic?

We use the best copywriting! Exaggerated but not deceptive — after all, these are fictional products.

Does the app collect my data?

Absolutely not. We don't collect a single byte of data. Gameplay and progress stay on your device. Apple processes the optional in-app purchase.

Does the app have shake-to-ad functionality?

Some fictional ad challenges use shaking or tilting as game controls. They never open real product pages. Shake alternatives are available in accessibility settings.

How do I restore my Ad Immunity License?

Use Restore Purchases in Settings or on the license purchase screen, using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. The license is an optional one-time purchase. The three main modes and both stories are free.
