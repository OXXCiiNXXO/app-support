FAQ

Are these ads real?

No! All ad content is fictional satire meant for entertainment. If you really want those flying slippers, physics will need to make some breakthroughs first.

Why do some ads look so realistic?

We use the best copywriting! Exaggerated but not deceptive — after all, these are fictional products.

Does the app collect my data?

Absolutely not. We don't collect a single byte of data. Your privacy is as non-existent as our fictional products.

Does the app have shake-to-ad functionality?

No! On the contrary, this app explicitly states "🚫 No shake ads". You don't need to worry about being forced to jump to ad pages by shaking your phone.
