本地对战与存档

本地对战无需注册游戏账号或登录 Game Center。应用在设备上保存当前本地对局的规则版本、先手和走棋记录，用于再次打开时恢复棋局。无法正常恢复时，可能保留一份恢复副本。

应用不会主动把本地存档上传给开发者，也没有本地对局的跨设备同步功能。设备备份与恢复受系统设置控制。

演化沙盘的布局只用于当前实验，退出后不作为本地对局保存。

Game Center 远程对战

选择随机匹配后，应用使用 Apple Game Center 提供的登录、匹配和通信能力。应用在运行期间使用游戏范围内的玩家标识和显示昵称，以识别对手、分配阵营并显示对局双方。

对战中，双方通过 Game Center 交换必要的准备信息、布阵与移动操作，以及确认棋局一致的校验信息。对手可以看到你的 Game Center 昵称和本局操作。

本游戏没有自建账号、玩家数据库或开发者运营的游戏服务器，不建立联网战绩档案。应用只在设备上保留一个“上次联网对局尚未完成”的标记，用于意外退出后的中断提示；它不包含可恢复的联网棋盘。

Apple 自行运营 Game Center，并按其政策处理账号及服务活动信息。你可以在系统的 Game Center 设置中管理相关隐私选项。请同时阅读 Apple 的 Game Center 与隐私说明。

本游戏不要求你向开发者提供 Apple 账号密码或验证码。

技术支持与网站

当你主动发送支持邮件时，CiiN 会收到你提供的邮箱地址、问题说明和可选附件，并用于回复及排查问题。请只提供解决问题所需的信息；如需删除已提供的资料，可通过同一邮箱提出请求。

支持邮件会在支持邮箱中保留，以便处理问题和后续沟通。我们会根据删除请求及适用的保留要求处理相关资料，不将这些信息用于广告或出售。邮件的传输与存储也受所用邮件服务的政策约束。

本页与技术支持页沿用 CiiN 的 GitHub Pages 网站，不加入表单、统计脚本或浏览器存储。托管服务可能处理 IP 地址和访问日志等请求信息，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。主动打开外部链接时，对应服务适用自己的隐私政策。

其他数据使用

当前游戏未加入广告、第三方行为统计或跨应用追踪功能。游戏功能不请求访问通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或精确位置。

当前版本没有皮肤购买或订阅。将来增加相关功能或改变数据用途时，我们会相应更新说明。

保留方式与选择

  • 可以只玩本地对战，不进入远程匹配。
  • 确认重新开局会替换当前本地对局。要清除这台设备上的全部应用数据，可在系统设置中选择「删除 App」；设备备份中的资料由系统备份设置管理。
  • 退出联网对局会结束当前连接。本作不提供跨重启继续旧联网局的功能，也不因中断自动判胜负。
  • Game Center 账号、活动分享及 Apple 持有的信息由 Apple 的相关设置和隐私流程管理，不能通过清除本游戏存档一并删除。

本游戏没有可单独注销的自建账号。技术支持和隐私请求请联系 oxxciinxxo@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This policy describes information handling by CiiN's Game of Life strategy game, 生命游戏棋, and these support pages. It covers the current 0.1.0 development version and will be updated if a release changes these practices.

Local play

Local play requires no game account or Game Center sign-in. Rules version, first player, and move history are stored on your device to restore the current local match. A recovery copy may be retained if a save cannot be restored. The app does not upload local saves to the developer or sync them across devices. System backups depend on your device settings. Sandbox layouts are temporary.

Game Center

Remote play uses Apple's Game Center for authentication, matchmaking, and communication. The app uses game-scoped player identifiers and display names during the session to identify players and assign sides. Players exchange setup information, moves, and consistency checks through Game Center. Your opponent can see your Game Center nickname and match actions.

The developer operates no game account system, player database, or game server and keeps no online match history. A local unfinished-match flag enables an interruption notice after an unexpected exit; it is not a recoverable online game. Apple operates Game Center under its own Game Center privacy information. The developer does not receive your Apple Account password or verification codes.

Support and website

If you email CiiN, your address, message, and optional attachments are used to respond and investigate. Support correspondence remains in the support mailbox for handling the issue and follow-up. You may request deletion by email; requests are handled subject to applicable retention requirements. This information is not sold or used for advertising. Your email service also governs email transmission and storage.

These pages use CiiN's existing GitHub Pages site without adding forms, analytics scripts, or browser storage. Hosting may process request information such as IP addresses and access logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External services have their own privacy policies.

Other practices and choices

The current app has no advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, skin purchases, or subscriptions. Gameplay does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location access.

You may use local play without matchmaking. Starting a new local game replaces the current match. Choose Delete App in system settings to remove the app's data from that device; manage backups separately. Online matches are not restored across restarts, and interruptions do not award a win. Manage Game Center account data and activity sharing through Apple's settings and privacy processes. The app has no separate developer account to delete.

Changes will be posted here with an updated date. Contact CiiN at oxxciinxxo@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.
