联系支持

有购买、存档或游玩问题，欢迎邮件联系 oxxciinxxo@gmail.com。请说明设备型号、iOS/iPadOS 版本、游戏版本、发生了什么，以及如何再次遇到问题。可附截图，但请遮住账号、付款信息和私人照片。

支持哪些设备和语言？

支持 iOS 17 或更新版本的 iPhone，以及 iPadOS 17 或更新版本的 iPad，界面适配横竖屏和较大的平板画面。

在设置中的“语言 / Language”选择简体中文、English 或跟随系统。跟随系统时，系统首选语言为中文则显示简体中文；其他语言（包括法语）显示英语。

需要联网或注册吗？

游玩和本地保存无需网络或游戏账号。iCloud 同步、购买、恢复购买及查询价格需要连接 Apple 服务。若所选照片仅在 iCloud 照片中，系统需要联网取得它。游戏没有广告、会员订阅或付费金币充值。

怎样保存和换设备？

游戏会自动保存。离开前可点右上角普通暂停，再选“保存并回到首页”。换设备前，在 iCloud 牧场存档页点“现在同步”，确认同步状态后，再用同一 iCloud 账户在另一台设备打开游戏首页。

两台设备都新增了进度时，请在首页的 iCloud 存档页选择想继续的一整份。不会把两份绒毛和物件自动相加；最近一次被替换的存档会留在当前设备，可点“找回上一次替换前的存档”。不同设备同时游玩时，建议每次换机前先同步。

恢复购买恢复的是付费内容权限；羊群、绒毛、克隆图片和布置需要 iCloud 存档。商店购买用的 Apple 账户可以与保存牧场的 iCloud 账户不同。

内容包怎样收费和恢复？

小帮手包、花园小院、山谷远足均为一次购买永久解锁，当前美国区价格各为 US$0.99。实际地区、币种和金额以 Apple 购买确认界面为准，无自动续费。

小帮手包包含花瓣风、同伴花结和时空暂停；购买前每种可免费试用 3 次，时空暂停每次试用最长 30 秒。购买后可持续使用，技能本身的冷却与效果时长仍适用。花园小院提供牧场外观与摆件，山谷远足提供两条额外路线。

使用原购买时的商店 Apple 账户，在内容包页面或“关于”中点“恢复购买”。旧版有效的时空暂停或小帮手包购买可解锁完整小帮手包。若商店暂时不可用，稍后联网重试即可；恢复购买不需要再付一次款。

绒毛、小帮手和克隆羊怎么用？

绒毛通过游玩获得，不提供现金充值。可用来永久学习呼唤、叼起、一袋嫩草（各 3 团），或反复制作牧场物件。旧档已经学会的小帮手继续保留，轻吠始终可用。

野餐垫、喂鸟台、雨中小屋分别需要 3、6、9 团绒毛，并保留各自的首次解锁条件。每件可独立摆放、叼起或收回；栅栏一段 3 团，门一扇 30 团。任务奖励仍可获得材料。

第一次成功创建克隆羊品种需要 6 团绒毛，以后每种 30 团。选一张照片、涂抹保留想要的形状并起名字，最后确认成功才扣除绒毛，首次迎接这个新品种的 3 只羊。取消、处理失败或失败的确认不收费。已经按旧价成功制作过品种的存档会一次性补回 24 团绒毛。

克隆羊品种与模样会长期保留，即使暂时没有活着的同品种羊，也不会删除收藏或重置首次价格。可以在羊种设置中选择下一批羊舍同伴及野羊的品种来源。

怎样控制、布置和照料牧场？

在草地上拖动控制边牧。右下角的大按钮使用当前小帮手，小按钮打开切换界面。轻吠驱赶和救羊，呼唤领路，嫩草小路能引诱并逐渐驯服野羊；右上角是普通暂停。

在“布置牧场”中选择物件，再到草地上放置；可收起物件面板继续布置下方草地。已学会叼起后，也能搬动羊舍、树、石头、花、展示牌和栅栏。

三只同品种、已驯服的成年羊聚在一起可以回到羊舍并留下小花；野羊未驯服前不参与聚拢或繁殖。照料花甸、喂鸟台和草地，能迎来访客。不同设施可以各自来访，小狗一次最多带领一只鸟和一只蝴蝶。

为什么音乐有时停下来？

这是有意设计的安静时段。进入游戏前 30 秒不播放背景音乐，三首曲子按顺序播放，每首之间随机留出 1～2 分钟的空档，轻柔游戏音效仍可保留。各模式都遵循这个节奏，设置中可分别控制音乐、音效和触感。

如何保存或分享明信片？

在明信片界面调整取景后，可以保存图片到系统照片图库，或主动打开分享界面。只有选择保存到照片时才请求添加权限。拒绝该权限不影响牧场游玩或照片选图；也可使用分享方式保存到其他位置。请先检查图片中是否有不希望分享的克隆照片或名字。

重开、清除数据或同步异常怎么办？

重开一局会保留长期收藏、已学会技能、绒毛和克隆品种，不是完全清空。系统“删除 App”可清除本机应用数据；iCloud 副本和购买记录需分别管理。完整的本地、云端及导出图片删除说明见 隐私政策。

同步失败时，先确认各设备使用同一 iCloud 账户、已允许本应用使用 iCloud、网络可用且云空间足够。回到首页重试“现在同步”。系统设置路径见 Apple 的 iCloud 说明。

出现问题请优先保存并回首页，重新打开游戏；仍有问题可重启设备或联系支持。在未确认云端已同步前，不要以删除重装作为首个排查步骤，以免丢失仅保存在本机的进度。

Contact support

Email oxxciinxxo@gmail.com for purchase, save, or gameplay help. Include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, game version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots help, but hide account details, payment information, and private photos.

Devices and language

The game supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later and iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, with layouts for portrait, landscape, and larger tablet screens.

In Settings, choose 简体中文, English, or System under “语言 / Language”. System uses Simplified Chinese when the first preferred system language is Chinese; other languages, including French, fall back to English.

Internet and accounts

Play and local saves need neither an internet connection nor a game account. iCloud sync, purchases, restoration, and prices connect to Apple services. If a selected picture exists only in iCloud Photos, the system needs a connection to retrieve it. There are no ads, membership subscriptions, or paid currency top-ups.

Saving and changing devices

The game saves automatically. Before leaving, open the top-right pause menu and choose Save & Return Home. Before changing devices, open iCloud Meadow Save, use Sync Now, and check its status. Then open the title screen on the other device using the same iCloud account.

If both devices have new progress, choose one complete save on the title screen’s iCloud page. Wool and objects from two saves are not added together. The most recently displaced save stays on the current device and can be recovered with the backup button. Sync before each device switch when playing on more than one device.

Restore Purchases restores paid access. Flocks, wool, custom pictures, and layouts need the iCloud save. The App Store purchasing account may differ from the iCloud account used for your meadow.

Content packs and restoration

The Helpers Pack, Garden Cottage, and Valley Adventure are each a one-time permanent purchase, currently US$0.99 in the US storefront. Apple’s purchase confirmation shows the actual regional price and currency. There is no automatic renewal.

The Helpers Pack contains Petal Breeze, Flock Blossom, and Time Pause. Each has 3 free trial uses before purchase; each Time Pause trial lasts up to 30 seconds. Purchase allows continued use while the helpers’ normal cooldowns and effect durations still apply. Garden Cottage adds meadow styles and decorations; Valley Adventure adds two routes.

Use the same App Store account that made the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases in the content-pack page or About. A valid original Time Pause or Helpers purchase unlocks the complete Helpers Pack. If Apple’s store is temporarily unavailable, try again online later. Restoring does not charge you again.

Wool, helpers, and custom breeds

Wool is earned through play and cannot be bought with cash. Permanently learn Call, Carry, and Grass Trail for 3 tufts each, or repeatedly craft meadow objects. Helpers already learned in an older save stay learned. Bark is always available.

A picnic blanket, bird feeder, and rain shelter cost 3, 6, and 9 tufts respectively, with their existing first-unlock conditions. Each crafted object can be placed, carried, or stored independently. A fence section costs 3 tufts and a gate 30. Job rewards still provide materials.

Your first successfully created custom breed costs 6 tufts; each later breed costs 30. Select a photo, brush the shape to keep, and give it a name. Wool is spent only on successful final confirmation, which welcomes the new breed’s initial 3 sheep. Cancellation, image-processing failure, or a failed confirmation costs nothing. Saves that successfully created a breed at the previous price receive a one-time 24-tuft credit.

A custom breed’s name and appearance remain in your collection even when none of its sheep are currently alive. This does not reset the first-craft price. Breed settings let you choose the pool for future sheepfold arrivals and wild visitors.

Controls and meadow care

Drag on the grass to move the collie. The large bottom-right button uses your current helper; the small button opens the helper switcher. Bark guides and rescues sheep, Call leads, and Grass Trail attracts and gradually tames wild sheep. The top-right control is ordinary pause.

In Arrange Meadow, choose an object and place it on the grass. Collapse the object palette to reach the lower meadow. Once Carry is learned, you can also move the sheepfold, trees, rocks, flowers, board, and fences.

Three tame adults of the same breed can gather home and leave a flower. Untamed wild sheep do not gather home or breed. Tending flower patches, feeders, and grass attracts visitors. Habitats can receive separate visits, while the collie can guide at most one bird and one butterfly at a time.

Quiet music intervals

The pauses are intentional. Background music stays quiet for the first 30 seconds of play. Three tracks then play in sequence with a random 1–2 minute musical rest between them; gentle game sounds can remain. Every mode follows this rhythm. Music, effects, and haptics have separate settings.

Postcards and photo access

Choose your framing in the postcard view, then save to Photos or open the share sheet. The game requests add-only Photos permission only when you choose to save there. Denying it does not block play or selecting a photo for a custom breed; sharing can save elsewhere. Review custom pictures and names before sharing.

Restarting, deletion, and save problems

Starting another round keeps long-term collections, learned helpers, wool, and custom breeds. Delete App in system settings clears local app data, while the iCloud copy and purchase records are managed separately. See the Privacy Policy for local, cloud, and exported-image deletion details.

If sync fails, confirm the same iCloud account on both devices, allow this app to use iCloud, and check connectivity and cloud storage. Return to the title screen and try Sync Now. See Apple’s iCloud settings guide.

For other issues, first save and return home, then reopen the game. Restart the device or contact support if needed. Avoid deleting and reinstalling as the first troubleshooting step until you have confirmed a successful cloud sync, because progress may exist only locally.
